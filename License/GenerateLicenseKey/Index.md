## Purpose

This section explains how to get a trial or paid license key. You can do this from the TYPO3 backend (T3Planet Shop) or from the T3Planet website.

## Get Trial License from Backend

Starting a free trial is easier now. Go to **T3Planet Shop** in the TYPO3
backend, click **Get New License**, pick your extension, and activate the trial
from there.

### Demo

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmshc993i0pb3qmaaoqrn3oma?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Get Trial License from Backend" allow="clipboard-write" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### Steps

1. Open TYPO3 Backend and go to `[T3Planet Shop]` in the module menu.
2. Click `[Get New License]`.
3. Click `[Get Started]`.
4. Search for the extension (for example `All in One Feedback`) and select it from the list.
5. Click `[Continue]`.
6. Enter your full name in the `[Name]` field.
7. Tick the checkbox to agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
8. Click `[Send Code]`. You will get a verification code by email.
9. Enter the code and click `[Verify & Activate]`.
- If the email is missing, check your spam/junk folder.
10. Click `[Activate license]`.

### Result

After verification, your trial license is activated. You can manage it from
T3Planet Shop.

<Note>
Please use a valid email address. The verification code is required to activate
the trial.
</Note>

## Purchase License from Backend

Buying a license is also possible from the backend now. In **T3Planet Shop**,
open **Get New License**, select your extension, click **Buy Now**, and finish
the payment.

### Demo

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmshcdtkx0pe3qmaa5qqg8dw1?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Purchase License from Backend" allow="clipboard-write" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### Steps

1. Open TYPO3 Backend and go to `[T3Planet Shop]`.
2. Click `[Get New License]`.
3. Click `[Get Started]`.
4. Search for the product (for example `AI Accessibility`) and select it.
5. Click `[Buy Now]`.
6. Click `[Continue]`.
7. Tick the checkbox to agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Click `[Proceed to payment]` and complete the payment.

### Result

After payment, your license is ready. Open T3Planet Shop to activate and manage it.

<Note>
Please keep the license email and payment confirmation. If the license is not
activated automatically, paste the license key in T3Planet Shop.
</Note>

<Tip>
If you already have a trial and want to upgrade, see
[Renewing or Purchasing a License](/License/RenewPurchase/Index).
</Tip>

## Generate License Key from Website

You can still get a trial license key from the T3Planet website and then activate
it in T3Planet Shop.

### Demo

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmn60qf154j64z3qmr0j91jdo?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Get Your News Comment License Key in Minutes" allow="clipboard-write" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### Steps

1. Click `[Get Started]` on the demo page.
2. Open the `[Products]` menu to explore available solutions.
3. Select the `[News Comment]` extension.
4. Click `[Start Free Trial]` to start your 30-day trial.
5. Scroll down and click `[Start Free Trial]` once more.
6. Review and accept the Privacy Policy and Terms to proceed.
- Fill in `[Name *]`, `[Email *]`, and `[Telephone (Optional)]`.
- Tick the agreement checkbox: `I have read and agree to T3Planet the Privacy Policy, License Terms, and Terms & Conditions. Your data is used solely to deliver your license key and provide product support - never shared or used for third-party promotions`.
7. Click `[Continue]` to move forward.
8. Enter your domains to configure your setup environment:
- `[Local Domain *]`
- `[Staging Domain]`
- `[Production Domain]`
- Note: `One license key. All three domains. No restrictions.`
9. Click `[Send verification code]` to request the verification code.
10. Enter the `[Verification code]` (6-digit boxes) and click `[Confirm & get my license key]`.
- If you cannot find the email/code, check your spam/junk folder (or use `[Resend code]` when available).
11. Close the window using the `[X]` button and continue.

### Result

After verification, your license key is generated (shown in the flow and/or delivered by email).
You can then activate it inside T3Planet Shop in TYPO3.

<Note>
Use the same domains you plan to use in TYPO3. Domain mismatch may prevent license activation.

If the verification email does not arrive, check your spam/junk folder.
</Note>

<Tip>
If you manage multiple environments, register local, staging, and production domains
during initial setup to avoid re-registration later.
</Tip>
