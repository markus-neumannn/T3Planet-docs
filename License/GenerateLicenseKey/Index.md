## Purpose

This section explains how to get a trial or paid license key. You can do this from the TYPO3 backend (T3Planet Shop) or from the T3Planet website.

<Note>
  **Where to find the module:** in TYPO3 v14 open **System → T3Planet Shop**. In TYPO3 v12 and v13 it is under **Admin Tools → T3Planet Shop**. T3Planet Shop is the module previously called License Manager.
</Note>

## Get New License from the backend

Trial and purchase are one wizard. Open **T3Planet Shop**, click **Get New License**, and follow five steps: **1 Welcome**, **2 Mode**, **3 Details**, **4 Verify**, **5 Done**. At step 2 you choose the product and then **Free Trial** or **Buy Now**.

### Free trial

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmshc993i0pb3qmaaoqrn3oma?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Get Trial License from Backend" allow="clipboard-write; fullscreen" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

1. Open **T3Planet Shop** and click `[Get New License]`.
2. **Welcome:** click `[Get Started]`.
3. **Mode:** search for your extension in `[Select a product]` (for example `All in One Feedback`), select the **Free Trial** card, and click `[Continue]`.
4. **Details:** fill in the form:
   - `[Email *]` (required): the address that receives your verification code.
   - `[Domain *]` (required): the single domain where you will use the extension. The field arrives pre-filled; check it before you continue. Enter one domain only, not a comma-separated list.
   - `[Name]` (optional).
   - Tick the checkbox to agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
5. Click `[Send Code]`. You receive a 6-digit code by email.
6. **Verify:** enter the code and confirm. If the email is missing, check your spam/junk folder or use `[Resend code]`.
7. **Done:** your trial license is activated. Manage it from T3Planet Shop.

<Note>
Use a valid email address. The verification code is required to activate
the trial.
</Note>

### Buy a license

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmshcdtkx0pe3qmaa5qqg8dw1?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Purchase License from Backend" allow="clipboard-write; fullscreen" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

1. Open **T3Planet Shop** and click `[Get New License]`.
2. **Welcome:** click `[Get Started]`.
3. **Mode:** search for the product (for example `AI Accessibility`), select the **Buy Now** card, and click `[Continue]`.
4. Tick the checkbox to agree to the terms and conditions and click `[Continue]`.
5. Complete the payment in the secure checkout on t3planet.shop.

After payment, your license is ready. Open T3Planet Shop to check that it is active.

<Note>
Please keep the license email and payment confirmation. If the license is not
activated automatically, paste the license key in T3Planet Shop.
</Note>

<Tip>
If you already have a trial and want to upgrade, see
[Renewing or Purchasing a License](/en/latest/License/RenewPurchase/Index).
</Tip>

## Generate License Key from Website

You can still get a trial license key from the T3Planet website and then activate
it in T3Planet Shop.

### Demo

<div className="t3-embed"><iframe src="https://app.supademo.com/embed/cmn60qf154j64z3qmr0j91jdo?embed_v=2&utm_source=embed" loading="lazy" title="Get Your News Comment License Key in Minutes" allow="clipboard-write; fullscreen" frameBorder="0" webkitallowfullscreen="true" mozallowfullscreen="true" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

### Steps

1. Click `[Get Started]` on the demo page.
2. Open the `[Products]` menu to explore available solutions.
3. Select the `[News Comment]` extension.
4. Click `[Start Free Trial]` to start your 30-day trial.
5. Scroll down and click `[Start Free Trial]` once more.
6. Review and accept the Privacy Policy and Terms to proceed.
   - Fill in `[Name *]`, `[Email *]`, and `[Telephone (Optional)]`.
   - Tick the agreement checkbox.
7. Click `[Continue]` to move forward.
8. Enter your domains to configure your setup environment: `[Local Domain *]`, `[Staging Domain]`, `[Production Domain]`. One license key covers your local, staging and production environments. Only production domains count towards your license tier.
9. Click `[Send verification code]` to request the verification code.
10. Enter the `[Verification code]` (6-digit boxes) and click `[Confirm & get my license key]`. If you cannot find the email, check your spam/junk folder or use `[Resend code]`.
11. Close the window using the `[X]` button and continue.

### Result

After verification, your license key is generated (shown in the flow and/or delivered by email).
You can then activate it inside T3Planet Shop in TYPO3.

<Note>
Use the same domains you plan to use in TYPO3. Domain mismatch may prevent license activation.
</Note>

<Tip>
If you manage multiple environments, register local, staging, and production domains
during initial setup to avoid re-registration later. You can add and change your domains yourself at any time in T3Planet Shop.
</Tip>
