## Purpose

The **All Licenses** tab in T3Planet Shop lists every license linked to your account. Use it to verify your email, view assigned domains, export license data, search and filter records, and sort by product, status, expiry date, and more.

## Open All Licenses

1. Open TYPO3 Backend and go to `[T3Planet Shop]` in the module menu.
2. Click the `[All Licenses]` tab.

## Verify your email

The first time you open **All Licenses**, confirm the email that owns the licenses.

1. Enter your email address in the verification field.
2. Click `[Send code]` to receive a one-time verification code.
3. Enter the code and click `[Verify]`.

<Note>
Use a valid email address. If the code does not arrive, check your spam/junk folder.
</Note>

## View assigned domains

1. Click `[View domains]` on a license row to see the domains bound to that license.
2. Close the associated domains dialog when you are done.

## Export, search, and refresh

1. Click `[Export CSV]` to download all license information as a spreadsheet.
2. Use the search field to filter licenses by key, extension, or domain.
3. Click `[Refresh]` to reload the latest license information.

## Sort the license table

Click a column header to sort the list. Click again to reverse the order.

Available columns include:

- `[Product]`
- `[License key]`
- `[Latest Ext Version]`
- `[Expiry Date]`
- `[Status]`
- `[Production domain]`
- `[Actions]`

## Result

After verification, **All Licenses** shows every license for your account. You can export the list, search by key, extension, or domain, inspect assigned domains, and sort by product, version, expiry date, status, or production domain.
