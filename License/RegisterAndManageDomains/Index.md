You can add and manage domains directly from T3Planet Shop. This lets you control where the extension is used without changing anything during deployment.

Whether you are working with staging, production, or multiple domains, you can update domain access anytime from the module.

## Step-by-step Instructions

1. Open the Manage Domains section to view and configure your active domains.
2. Use the search field to quickly find your domain.
3. Filter domains by environment to narrow down results.
4. Click `Add Domain` to register a new domain for your site.
5. Enter your domain name without `http` or `https`.
6. Select the appropriate environment from the dropdown.
7. Confirm by clicking `Add Domain` to complete the setup.
8. Open the `Authentication Logs` tab to view activity records.
9. Use the search field to filter and find specific log entries.
10. Close the window anytime by clicking `Cancel`.
