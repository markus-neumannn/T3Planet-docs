Custom LLM Support provides a complete and scalable solution for integrating AI into TYPO3. From data ingestion and semantic embedding to vector search and secure API delivery, the system ensures high-performance retrieval, privacy-focused processing, and continuous updates. By leveraging modern open-source or enterprise-grade models, the solution adapts to websites of all sizes—from small portals to large content-heavy platforms with tens of thousands of pages.
