Here are the requirements to install, configure and use T3AI. T3AI follows the AI Universe range of AI Foundation.

- TYPO3 v12 – v14 (12.4 LTS, 13.4 LTS, 14.x)
- PHP 8.2 or higher
- EXT:backend
- EXT:filelist
- EXT:dashboard
- EXT:fluid
- EXT:seo
- EXT:ns_t3af
- EXT:ns_license
