![TYPO3-Revolution-Slider-Banner-2](./images/TYPO3-Revolution-Slider-Banner-2.webp)

## What does it do?

Are you looking to utilize the full visual editing power with simple drag and drop functionality just like WordPress for your TYPO3 website without any manual configurations? You've got! Now, the world's most popular Slider Revolution is now available in TYPO3 CMS too.
