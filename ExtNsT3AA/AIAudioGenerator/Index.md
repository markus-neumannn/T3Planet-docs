**Steps to Generate AI Audio from Text:**

**Step 1** : Go to **T3AA Extension > Dashboard > AI Audio**.

**Step 2** : Choose your **AI model**, **voice type**, **speed**, and **output format**.

**Step 3** : Select the **target folder** for audio storage.

**Step 4** : Click **Generate AI Audio**.

**Alternative Method (via text and file):**

**Step 1** : Go to the desired **folder**.

**Step 2** : Select or upload a **text file**.

**Step 3** : Generate and download the AI audio file directly.
