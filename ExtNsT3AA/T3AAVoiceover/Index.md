With **T3AA Voiceover**, you can add AI-generated audio voiceovers to any TYPO3 page, enhancing accessibility and user engagement.

**Steps to Generate Voiceover for Pages:**

**Step 1** : Select the target TYPO3 page.

**Step 2** : Open the **AI Voiceover** tab in Page Properties.

**Step 3** : Choose your desired **AI model**, **tone**, **speed**, and **audio format**.

**Step 4** : Click **Generate AI Audio**.

**Step 5** : The generated voiceover will be saved to the page properties.

**Step 6** : Add the **T3AA Voiceover Plugin** in Page to render voice in the frontend.

**Step 7** : Customize and preview the voiceover output.

# AI Voiceover for Blog

Add engaging audio to your TYPO3 blog articles with just a few clicks.

**Steps to Generate Blog Voiceover:**

**Step 1** : Choose the blog page and open the **T3AA** tab then choose **AI Voiceove** tab.

**Step 2** : Select the preferred **AI model**, **tone**, **speed**, and **format**.

**Step 3** : Click **Generate AI Audio**.

**Step 4** : The audio file is automatically saved in the page properties.

**Step 5** : Add the **Voiceover Plugin**, customize the settings, and click **Save**.
