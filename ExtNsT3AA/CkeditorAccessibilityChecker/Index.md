T3AA’s integration with CKEditor helps you check and improve accessibility for both text and images directly inside the editor. This ensures your content stays clear, compliant, and user-friendly for everyone.

**Step 1** Add your content in the RTE (Rich Text Editor).

**Step 2:** Click the Accessibility Check option to scan your content and find potential issues.

**Step 3:** For images added in the RTE, the tool will highlight if alt text is missing.

**Step 4:** If alt text is missing, you can then use T3AA to generate and insert appropriate alt text.
