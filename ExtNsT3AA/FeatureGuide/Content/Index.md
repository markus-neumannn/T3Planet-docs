The **Content** tab shows feature cards for common accessibility content jobs
on the selected page (Live Audit, AI Alt Text / Filemeta, audio, voiceover,
simplify text, and similar). Use a card’s primary action to start that job.

## Steps

1. Configure an AI provider in **AI Foundation → AI Providers** (own keys or
   T3Planet Credits) for use of AI features.
2. Enable the relevant options under **AI Foundation → AI Features → T3AA**.
3. Open **AI Accessibility**.
4. Select the page in the page tree.
5. Open the **Content** tab.
6. Choose a feature card and use its primary action.
