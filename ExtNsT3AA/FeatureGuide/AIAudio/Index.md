**AI Audio** converts text or an uploaded script into downloadable AI-generated
audio files. This is separate from **AI Voiceover**, which attaches audio to a
page/blog and renders it with a frontend plugin.

## Steps to Generate AI Audio from Text

1. Open **AI Accessibility → Content** (or the AI Audio entry in
   your setup) and start **Create audio from a script**.
2. Choose **AI model**, **voice**, **speed**, and **output format**.
3. Select the **target folder** for the audio file.
4. Click **Generate AI Audio**.
5. Review and download the generated file.

## Alternative Method (via text and file)

1. Go to the desired **folder**.
2. Select or upload a **text file**.
3. Generate and download the AI audio file directly.
