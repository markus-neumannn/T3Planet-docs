**1. How do I open a Friendly Captcha account?**

Create a free account at [https://friendlycaptcha.com/](https://friendlycaptcha.com/) — click **Sign up** and follow the registration steps.

**2. How do I generate a Sitekey and Secret Key?**

See [FriendlyCaptcha Documentation](https://docs.friendlycaptcha.com/#/installation?id=_1-generating-a-sitekey) and the Configuration section of this manual.

**3. Can I test from a local server without an official domain?**

Yes — create a free account and generate a separate development Sitekey/Secret Key for local testing. This is the recommended approach.

**4. Can I use the same key for local and production?**

No. Use a dedicated development key locally and your production key on the live domain — production credentials are tied to registered domains.

**5. What is the license fee?**

The T3Planet extension is free. Friendly Captcha’s service has a free tier with a monthly limit; see [https://friendlycaptcha.com/#pricing](https://friendlycaptcha.com/#pricing) for paid plans.
