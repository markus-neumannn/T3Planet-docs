The TYPO3 AI Localization (T3AL) extension helps you translate your website faster and easier using AI. It automates repetitive tasks, saving time for developers, project managers, agencies, and marketers. With direct Crowdin integration, it keeps everything organized and smooth. Everyone can focus more on quality content instead of manual translation work.

Whether you're a developer, agency, project manager, or marketer, T3AL makes TYPO3 localization faster, smarter, and easier for everyone.
