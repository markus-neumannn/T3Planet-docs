# System Requirements

Here is the pre-requisite to install, configure, and use the T3AB TYPO3 AI extension.

- **TYPO3 Compatibility**: version 12 & 13
- **PHP Versions Supported**: >= 8.1
- **Required TYPO3 Extensions**:
  \- EXT:form
  \- EXT:content_blocks
  \- EXT:ns_license
