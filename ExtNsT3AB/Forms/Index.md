T3AB includes an intelligent **AI Form Builder**. Just describe your form (e.g., “Contact form with name, email, subject, message”), and T3AB will generate it.

Features:

- Generate forms for contact, feedback, registration, surveys
- Customize each field: type, label, validation
- Edit layout in TYPO3 backend
- Add/remove fields manually

Forms are fully compatible with TYPO3 Form Framework and ready for production.
